Technique rules

Rep log

Runs fully in-browser on MediaPipe's BlazePose (Heavy model, GPU-accelerated) — no video ever leaves your device. Your outline is traced live from your own detected joints — it adapts to your height and distance automatically, so there's no fixed spot you need to stand on. It shows yellow until your framing is right, then holds green briefly before the exercise starts. Rep angles are measured from MediaPipe's real-world 3D landmarks (metric, hip-centered) rather than flat screen coordinates, and lightly smoothed frame-to-frame to avoid jitter — the same approach Google documents in their own pose-classification pipeline. Floor exercises like push-ups work best with the phone propped low and angled to see your whole body side-on. If the camera doesn't start in an embedded preview, download this file and open it directly, or serve it from GitHub Pages / Cloudflare Pages.
